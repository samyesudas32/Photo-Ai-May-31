# **App Name**: PixelPass AI

## Core Features:

- Image Upload: Allows users to upload their portrait photograph for processing.
- Automated Face Detection & Alignment: Automatically detects, centers, and aligns the face according to passport standards (eyes level, head straight).
- Intelligent Background Replacement Tool: Utilizes AI to segment the subject and replace the background with a pure, uniformly lit white (#FFFFFF) background.
- AI-Powered Image Enhancement Tool: Analyzes and corrects lighting balance, exposure, sharpens details naturally, and removes harsh shadows/highlights while preserving identity.
- Subtle Skin Retouching Tool: Gently removes temporary blemishes, glare, and skin shine, ensuring natural skin texture is maintained without altering identity.
- High-Resolution Upscaling: Upscales the processed image to a target 4K resolution, ensuring print quality without losing realism.
- Passport Standard Formatting & Download: Formats the processed image to meet official passport standards (e.g., 2x2 inch, centered head) and enables convenient download.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary brand color: A professional, calming blue (#336699) to convey precision and reliability on a light background.
- Background color: A very light, desaturated grey-blue (#EBF0F2) providing a clean, spacious canvas for image display.
- Accent color: A vibrant, clear cyan-blue (#38AFD0) for highlighting key actions and call-to-action buttons, ensuring high visibility.
- All text: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for its modern, objective, and highly readable qualities, fitting the app's focus on accuracy.
- Use minimalist and universally recognizable icons for core actions like upload, process, and download, maintaining clarity.
- Implement a clean, spacious, two-column layout with a prominent image preview area on one side and controls/settings on the other, focusing user attention on the photo.
- Subtle, non-disruptive loading animations or progress indicators to inform the user during image processing, indicating system activity.
